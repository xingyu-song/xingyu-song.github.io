I study flow-based generative models and the connections between deterministic and stochastic dynamics.

Research

My research explores how probability flows generate data. I am interested in the relationship between deterministic and stochastic dynamics, and in building simple systems that capture complex behavior.

Generative dynamics
Flow-based generative modeling, probability transport, bridge matching, and stochastic process structure.
Physical simulation
Training measures, multi-scale partitions, neural surrogates, and bias reduction for discretized simulations.
Embodied vision
Action recognition, synthetic data generation, 3D pose representation, and game-engine-based augmentation.
